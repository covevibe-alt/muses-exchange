How it works

Predict the score of every Champions League match before kickoff — all 144 league-phase games, then every knockout leg through to the final. Points are worked out automatically when a match finishes. No admin, no spreadsheets.

Points per match

Exact score — you said 2–1, it finished 2–15
Right winner / correct result (draw) + right goal difference — you said 2–1, it finished 3–23
Right winner only — you said 2–1, it finished 3–02
Wrong result0

Call a draw and it finishes level and you always get at least 3 — the goal difference is 0, so it matches by definition. That's deliberate: draws land in roughly one game in four but almost nobody backs them, so the hardest call in football pays properly.

Round multiplier

The league phase is 144 of the season's 189 matches. Score every round the same and the final is worth half a percent of the pool — so match points are multiplied by the round:

League phase×1
Knockout play-offs×2
Round of 16×2
Quarter-final×3
Semi-final×4
Final×5

An exact score in the final is worth 25. It splits the season roughly half league phase, half knockout — so a strong autumn matters, but it can't win the thing on its own.

Knockout extras

  • Who goes through — every knockout tie is two legs. On the second leg you also pick who advances on aggregate. Play-offs and round of 16 +4, quarter-final +6, semi-final +8.
  • How the final is decided — 90 minutes, extra time or penalties, for +5.
  • +3 more if you call penalties and pick the team that wins the shootout.

Leg scores are the goals in that leg, extra time included. Shootout kicks never count as goals.

Season-long bonus picks

🏆 Your champion lifts the trophy+30
🥈 …or they reach the final and lose+10
📊 Team that finishes 1st in the league phase+10
⚽ Top scorer of the competition+15

All three are picked once, before the first kickoff, and stay hidden until the deadline. The near-miss on the champion is there on purpose: being nearly right should beat being completely wrong.

The fine print

  • Predictions save as you type and lock 30 minutes before kickoff (enforced on the server). Until then, hit Edit and change them as often as you like.
  • Everyone else's predictions stay hidden until kickoff. After that they appear on the match card, and you can tap any player in the ranking to see everything they've filled in.
  • Ties in the ranking: most exact scores, then most correct results.
  • Scores come from ESPN automatically — live while you have the page open, final results within a minute or two of the whistle.
